About

At the intersection of models and systems.

I’m a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Meta focused on AI infrastructure. My work centers on productionizing, scaling, profiling, and optimizing recommendation systems and their training stacks.

I studied at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), with coursework spanning deep learning, statistical machine learning, large-scale data mining, natural language processing, and related areas.

More recently, I’ve been working on the systems challenges behind generative recommendation: combining recommender-native sparse components with LLM-style architectures and building infrastructure that can train them efficiently in production.
